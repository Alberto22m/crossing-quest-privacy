# Crossing Quest — Privacy Policy

**English** · [Русский](#русский) · [Español](#español) · [Français](#français) · [Deutsch](#deutsch) · [العربية](#العربية) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Crossing Quest is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the game and the services built into it process.

## What we do not collect

- The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.
- We, the developer, do not run our own servers and do not receive your personal data.

## Your game progress

Progress (stars, crystals, heroes, settings) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, it is also saved in your own iCloud (key-value storage) so it can sync between your devices. Only your devices with the same Apple Account can read it; the developer has no access.

## Purchases and subscription

Crystal packs and the monthly "No Ads" subscription are sold through Apple. Apple processes payments; the game only receives a confirmation that a purchase is valid. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

## Advertising

The game shows ads from two networks:

- **Google AdMob** (Google LLC) — [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- **Yandex Advertising Network** (YANDEX LLC), including the AppMetrica SDK — [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/)

These services may collect, on their own behalf:

- device identifiers (the advertising identifier IDFA only if you allow tracking, and other device identifiers);
- advertising data (which ads were shown or tapped);
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- information about interaction with the app;
- diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

They use this data to show and measure ads, limit how often ads are shown and prevent fraud.

**Your choices**

- Tracking: the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. You can change it any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are less personalized.
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads, and only Google ads are used. You can change your choice in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices.
- The "No Ads" subscription removes ads between levels. Reward videos (for example, an extra life) are always optional: they are shown only when you tap the button yourself.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the Kids category.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Apple, Google or Yandex, please use the tools and contacts in their privacy policies. You can also reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in your device settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: email [rnane.1@icloud.com](mailto:rnane.1@icloud.com) or [open an issue in this repository](https://github.com/Alberto22m/crossing-quest-privacy/issues). Game support: [Support page](https://alberto22m.github.io/crossing-quest-privacy/support/).

## 简体中文

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

Crossing Quest 是一款适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的解谜游戏。本政策说明游戏及其内置服务会处理哪些数据。

### 我们不收集的内容

- 游戏没有账户，不会询问你的姓名、电子邮件、电话号码或通讯录。
- 我们作为开发者不运营自己的服务器，也不会收到你的个人数据。

### 你的游戏进度

进度（星星、水晶、英雄、设置）保存在你的设备上。如果你已登录 iCloud，进度还会保存在你自己的 iCloud（键值存储）中，以便在你的设备之间同步。只有使用同一 Apple 账户的设备才能读取这些数据，开发者无法访问。

### 购买与订阅

水晶礼包和每月"免广告"订阅通过 Apple 销售。付款由 Apple 处理，游戏只会收到购买有效的确认。我们永远看不到你的支付信息。你可以随时在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。

### 广告

游戏会展示来自两个广告网络的广告：

- **Google AdMob**（Google LLC）— [Google 隐私权政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=zh-CN)、[Google 如何使用合作伙伴应用中的数据](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=zh-CN)
- **Yandex Advertising Network**（YANDEX LLC），包括 AppMetrica SDK — [Yandex 隐私政策](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/)

这些服务可能会以其自身名义收集：

- 设备标识符（仅在你允许跟踪时收集广告标识符 IDFA，以及其他设备标识符）；
- 广告数据（展示或点击了哪些广告）；
- 根据 IP 地址推断的大致位置；
- 与应用互动的信息；
- 诊断数据（崩溃和性能数据）。

它们使用这些数据来展示和衡量广告、限制广告展示频率并防止欺诈。

**你的选择**

- 跟踪：游戏会通过 Apple 的"App 跟踪透明度"请求你的许可。你可以随时在 iPhone"设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪"中更改。如果你拒绝，仍会看到广告，但个性化程度较低。
- 在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，展示广告前会显示同意表单（Google User Messaging Platform），并且只使用 Google 广告。你可以在游戏中更改选择："设置 → 广告隐私选项"。
- "免广告"订阅会移除关卡之间的广告。奖励视频（例如获得额外一条命）始终是可选的：只有在你自己点按按钮时才会播放。

### 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不属于"儿童"类别。

### 你的权利

我们不保存任何关于你的个人数据，因此我们这里没有可供查看、更正或删除的内容。对于 Apple、Google 或 Yandex 处理的数据，请使用其隐私政策中提供的工具和联系方式。你也可以在设备设置中重置广告标识符或限制跟踪。

### 变更

如果本政策发生变更，新版本将发布在此页面并注明新的生效日期。
